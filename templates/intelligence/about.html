About Extension Intelligence

Extension Intelligence (ExtensionCenter Intelligence) is a Chrome Extension market intelligence platform. We track publicly visible Chrome Web Store data every day — user counts, ratings, review counts, rankings, update frequency — and turn it into a searchable, comparable, downloadable historical dataset.

What we do

  • Market intelligence site: search and browse extensions, view growth rankings, filter for market opportunities
  • Chrome Extension Dataset: a daily-updated, complete historical dataset for researchers, investors, and competitive analysis teams to download
  • Custom research: custom market research requests for Custom Intelligence plan customers

Where the data comes from

Our data comes from regularly collecting publicly available Chrome Web Store pages, and does not involve any end-user personal information. For exact field sources and calculation methods, see the Methodology and Data Dictionary pages.
