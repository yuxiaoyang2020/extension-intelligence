What information we collect

We only collect information actually needed to provide the service — we don't collect data we have no use for:

  • Account information: the email address and password you provide when signing up (passwords are stored encrypted — we can't see the plaintext either). If you sign in with a Google account, we obtain your email, name, and avatar from Google to create or link your account.
  • Subscription and payment information: when you upgrade to a paid plan, payment information (card numbers, etc.) is processed directly by Stripe — we never see or store your full card details. We only store the customer ID, subscription ID, plan, and billing period dates that Stripe returns, to determine your account's access level.
  • Usage data: your actions on the site (such as searches and filter selections) are currently only used to process that specific request — we don't do behavioral analytics or third-party tracking. We currently have no analytics tools like Google Analytics or PostHog integrated; if we add one in the future, we'll update this section and notify you in advance.
  • Custom research requests: if you're a Custom Intelligence plan customer and submit a custom research request form, we store the title, description, and desired output format you provide, along with any files you upload.
  • Cookies: we only use session cookies necessary to keep you logged in and to prevent cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks — we don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use this information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the service (for example, determining how much data you can see based on your plan)
  • To process subscriptions and billing (via Stripe)
  • To respond to your support inquiries and custom research requests
  • To send you notifications directly related to your account/subscription (such as subscription status changes) — we will not send marketing emails without your consent

What we share, and with whom

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. To operate the service, we share the minimum necessary information with the following service providers:

  • Stripe (payment processing) — handles your subscriptions and payments
  • Google (if you choose Google sign-in) — identity verification
  • Alibaba Cloud (cloud servers and OSS object storage) — hosts the website and data files

If legally required (for example, by a court order), we may need to disclose information, but we will attempt to notify you in advance where legally permitted.

The Chrome Extension dataset itself

The Chrome Extension market dataset we sell (the Customer Dataset) is collected from publicly visible Chrome Web Store extension information (name, developer, user count, rating, etc.) and does not contain any end-user personal information — this dataset is a separate matter from "information we collect about you," and the two should not be confused.

Data retention

We retain the information above for as long as your account is active. You can contact us by email at any time to request deletion of your account and related data (custom research request attachments, subscription history, etc.), except where retention is legally required (for example, billing records tied to completed transactions).

These requests are currently handled manually — we don't yet have a self-service page for them.
